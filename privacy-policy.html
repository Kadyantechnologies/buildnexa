Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

At BuildNexa ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our enterprise clients and users. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when utilizing our architectural and estimation platform.

1. Information Collection

We collect enterprise-level information necessary to provide our services effectively:

  • Account Data: Corporate email addresses, names, role assignments, and organizational details.
  • Project Data: Uploaded blueprints, architectural drawings, estimations, material specifications, and client portfolio details processed through our platform.
  • Usage Data: Analytical telemetry regarding how our AI tools and estimation features are utilized to improve model accuracy.

2. Data Utilization

The information we collect is strictly used for the following purposes:

  • Providing, maintaining, and scaling the BuildNexa platform.
  • Enhancing the accuracy of our AI-driven drafting and cost estimation algorithms.
  • Processing transactions and sending related information, including enterprise billing.
  • Ensuring compliance, security, and preventing fraudulent activities.

3. Enterprise Data Security

We employ industry-standard, military-grade encryption (AES-256) for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. Your proprietary blueprints and cost estimation models remain strictly confidential and are isolated within your enterprise tenant environment.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, trade, or rent your proprietary data to external entities. Data may only be shared with verified sub-processors (e.g., cloud hosting providers) bound by strict confidentiality agreements to facilitate our service delivery.

5. Contact Information

For any privacy-related inquiries, data subject access requests, or compliance matters, please direct your communication to info@kadixgroup.com.
